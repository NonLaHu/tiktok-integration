Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Short version

TikTok Integration is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from other people. It has no analytics, no ads, and no public database.

2. What it can access

After the owner approves access through TikTok Login Kit, the tool can access:

  • The owner's OAuth access token and refresh token.
  • The owner's basic profile, such as display name, avatar, and open ID.
  • The owner's own list of videos.
  • Video files the owner chooses to upload to the owner's own account.

It does not access data belonging to other TikTok users.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer in a file that only the owner can read. Profile data and video details are processed on that computer. Video files are sent only to TikTok's official API when the owner starts an upload.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The only outside service the tool talks to is TikTok's official API.

5. How long data is kept

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Video files and details are not kept after the requested upload. Local logs do not contain passwords or tokens.

6. Security and revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions. The owner can also delete the local token file.

7. Children

This tool is not for children and is not intended for anyone other than the account owner.

8. Changes

This policy may change. The effective date above will be updated when it does.
